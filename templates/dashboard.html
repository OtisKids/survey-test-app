Scientific Sources & References

  • Wellbeing Assessment Methodology

    Our daily wellbeing assessment is based on validated measures from positive psychology research.

    Diener, E., Emmons, R. A., Larsen, R. J., & Griffin, S. (1985). The Satisfaction with Life Scale. Journal of Personality Assessment, 49, 71-75.

  • Anxiety Measurement

    Our anxiety assessment incorporates elements from the GAD-7, a clinically validated tool for measuring anxiety.

    Spitzer, R. L., Kroenke, K., Williams, J. B., & Löwe, B. (2006). A brief measure for assessing generalized anxiety disorder: the GAD-7. Archives of internal medicine, 166(10), 1092-1097.

  • Sleep Quality Assessment

    Our sleep quality survey is adapted from the Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index (PSQI).

    Buysse, D. J., Reynolds, C. F., Monk, T. H., Berman, S. R., & Kupfer, D. J. (1989). The Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index: a new instrument for psychiatric practice and research. Psychiatry research, 28(2), 193-213.
